About Us

📅 We opened recently – 10th February 2026 and will be fully operational from the upcoming academic session. Our vision is to nurture young minds with modern education rooted in Indian values.

Indian Public School, located in the serene village of Rasulpur Dabheri, is dedicated to providing quality education from Nursery to 8th standard. We believe every child is unique and deserves a holistic environment that promotes intellectual, social, and emotional growth. Our school is designed to be a second home where children feel safe, inspired, and motivated to learn.

With a focus on conceptual learning, co-curricular activities, and character development, we aim to build responsible citizens of tomorrow. The school is managed by experienced educators who prioritize child-centric pedagogy.
